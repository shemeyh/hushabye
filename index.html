Phone test

Safe sleep comes first

  • Always on the back, on a flat, firm surface.
  • Nothing loose in the bassinet: no pillows, blankets, bumpers or toys.
  • This is a soother, not a baby monitor. It cannot tell why a baby is crying, and it does not replace you.

Where things go

  • Speaker: at least 2 m from the baby. Never in or on the bassinet.
  • Phone: near the bassinet but out of reach, with its charging cable well away from the baby. Never in the bassinet.
  • Nothing gets attached to the bassinet.
  • Keep Hushabye on screen all night. Do not press the power button or switch to another app: the phone stops the microphone within seconds. Use Night screen for a black display.
  • Do Not Disturb on. Otherwise calls and notifications play through the speaker.
  • Set the phone's media volume and the speaker's volume where you will leave them. Changing either later changes how loud everything is.

Allow the microphone

Hushabye listens for crying through this phone's microphone. The sound is analysed on the phone and never recorded or sent anywhere.

Set the volume cap

        
    
          
  1. Open a free sound-meter app on a second phone, set to dBA (A-weighted).
    2. 
          
  2. Hold it where the baby's head will be.
    3. 
          
  3. Play the loudest level and move the cap until the meter reads 45 to 50 dBA. Never more than 50.
    4. 
        

        
You should clearly hear a rumble with a soft shush on top, coming from the speaker. If you hear nothing, or cannot reach 45 with the cap at 100%, turn up the phone's media volume or the speaker, then set the cap again.

        
Phone meters are approximate, and deep rumble reads lower in dBA than it really is. 50 dBA is the limit hospital nurseries use.

        

          

            
            
          

          
          

            Sound out
            

          

          
If this bar moves but you hear nothing, the sound is leaving the app and getting lost on the way to the speaker: check the Bluetooth connection and both volumes.

        

        
No level, music or voice can go above this cap. If you change the phone or speaker volume later, do this step again.

      


      

        
First nights: shadow mode

        
Shadow mode is on. The app plays the baseline sound, listens, and writes down what it would have done, but it does not raise the volume.

        
Check the log in the morning. When the cry detection matches what really happened, turn shadow mode off in Settings.

        
Before the first night, run the Phone test page once. It checks that this phone keeps listening with the screen dimmed and what happens if the speaker disconnects.
